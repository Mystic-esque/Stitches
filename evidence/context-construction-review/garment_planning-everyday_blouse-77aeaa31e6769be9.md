## Role in this knowledge base

The everyday blouse is kept as an explicitly **illustrative** graph example. It is not a planning candidate like the Sandy circle skirt or the Tina faux-wrap top (see `garment_planning/sandy_circle_skirt` and `garment_planning/tina_faux_wrap_top`). Its geometry serves only to demonstrate how Stitches models piece relationships, edge names, and fold/mirror cutting decisions — no cut-ready geometry or production pattern is derived from it. Do not use this pattern for actual garment construction.

Construction steps (if any) are out of scope here; see `construction/everyday_blouse`.
