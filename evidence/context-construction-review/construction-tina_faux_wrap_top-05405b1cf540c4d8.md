# Tina Faux-Wrap Top Construction Sequence

Eight ordered construction steps for the Tina top: sleeve markings, optional dart, shoulder seams, neckline binding, flat sleeve setting, front overlap study, side/underarm seams, and finish planning

Eight ordered construction steps for the Tina faux-wrap top, sourced from FreeSewing Tina top documentation. ## Step details

### 1 · Read asymmetric markings
Ordinal 1. Prerequisite for all subsequent steps. Condition: always. *(Step record referenced by step 2 as its prerequisite; not supplied as a standalone source — consult FreeSewing Tina instructions for specifics.)*

### 2 · Understand the optional dart
Ordinal 2. Condition: **dart** (applies only when your chosen pattern version includes a dart). Action: study the source instructions before choosing any alternative treatment. Checkpoint: confirm whether your chosen pattern actually has a dart. Orientation: confirm dart presence on the **front** piece. Prerequisite: step 1 (read asymmetric markings). [1]

### 3 · Join the shoulder seams
Ordinal 3. Condition: always. Action: sew both shoulders with a stretch-compatible seam. Orientation: front and back right sides together. Checkpoint: identify which edges meet and which remain open. Prerequisite: step 2 (understand the optional dart). [2]

### 4 · Follow the neckline binding
Ordinal 4. Condition: always. Action: study how binding ends later enter side or waist finishing — this determines where they are secured. Orientation: binding meets the diagonal fronts and back neck, right sides together. Pieces: front and back. Prerequisite: step 3 (shoulder seams). [3]

### 5 · Place the flat sleeves
Ordinal 5. Condition: **sleeves** (branch applies when sleeves are included). Action: match the x-notch to back and the round notch to front. Orientation: right sides together, sleeve hem points toward the neck opening. Checkpoint: confirm the intended notch pairing before stitching. Prerequisite: step 4 (neckline binding). [4]

### 6 · Understand overlap and lower front
Ordinal 6. Condition: **front-bottom** (applies when lower-front piece is present). Action: study the overlap before joining the lower-front seam. Orientation: visible outer front faces the lower-front piece. Checkpoint: determine which front layer should remain visible. Prerequisite: step 5 (flat sleeves). [5]

### 7 · Connect sides and underarms
Ordinal 7. Condition: always. Action: sew side seams and, when sleeves are present, sleeve underseams. Orientation: front and back right sides together. Checkpoint: verify that all included conditional branches (dart, sleeves, front-bottom) lead here without a missing step. Pieces: front, back, and sleeve when applicable. Prerequisite: step 6 (overlap and lower front). [6]

### 8 · Plan waist and arm finishes
Ordinal 8. Condition: always. Action: resolve each finish using your selected source version's instructions. Orientation: arm finish depends on whether sleeves are included; waist has alternative finishes. Checkpoint: identify which finish method and measurements still need maker review. Prerequisite: step 7 (sides and underarms). [7]

## Prerequisite chain

Steps form a strict linear chain: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8. Each step lists only its immediate predecessor as a prerequisite. Conditional steps (dart, sleeves, front-bottom) must be confirmed against your chosen pattern version before beginning the sequence. [8]
