# Sandy Circle Skirt Construction Sequence

Ordered construction steps for the Sandy circle skirt: closure seam, opening orientation, straight waistband attachment, waistband finishing and fastening, hem finish options, and pending-review status

The Sandy circle skirt construction plan (`companion-making-sandy-v1`) covers a non-seamless skirt with a straight overlapping waistband and a closure placed below the band [6]. It excludes lining, gathering, pockets, and curved waistband variations [6]. All five steps carry **pending** review status — this plan has not been physically reviewed and supports education and planning only, not cut-ready making [6].

For Sandy piece specifications and planning measurements, see `garment_planning/sandy_circle_skirt`. For seam puckering diagnostics, see `seam_quality`.

## Construction sequence

### Step 1 — Understand the closure seam (ordinal 1)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study the seam that ends at the opening; this plan excludes seamless skirts [1]. Right sides face each other [1]. Relevant piece: `skirt-default`; relevant edge: `sandy.skirt/opening` [1].

**Checkpoint:** Can you point to the opening that must stay accessible? [1]

---

### Step 2 — Plan the opening (ordinal 2)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes. **Prerequisite:** Step 1.

This study uses a closure placed below the waistband, not a zipper running through it [2]. The opening remains above the closed seam [2]. Piece: `skirt-default` [2].

**Checkpoint:** If your intended closure falls outside this study, pause rather than guess [2].

---

### Step 3 — Connect skirt and straight band (ordinal 3)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes. **Prerequisite:** Step 2.

Study the straight-band attachment and its overlap [5]. Curved bands, lining, and gathering require a different plan [5]. Orient with the interfaced band side and skirt right sides together [5]. Pieces: `skirt-default`, `waistband-straight`, `waistband-interfacing`; edges: `sandy.skirt/waist` and `sandy.waistband/attachment` [5].

**Checkpoint:** Identify the inside band edge and the overlap before proceeding [5].

---

### Step 4 — Understand the waistband finish (ordinal 4)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes. **Prerequisite:** Step 3.

Choose a source-supported overlap fastening; do not infer dimensions from the diagram [3]. The inside band edge covers the attachment point [3]. Piece: `waistband-straight` [3].

**Checkpoint:** Write down any unresolved closure or allowance detail before moving on [3].

---

### Step 5 — Compare hem finishes (ordinal 5)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes. **Prerequisite:** Step 4.

The source offers both a binding approach and a folded-hem approach; this study does not select an allowance [4]. Inspect the lower edge (`sandy.skirt/hem`), not the waist edge [4]. Piece: `skirt-default` [4].

**Checkpoint:** Be able to explain which finish you will ask a maker to check [4].

## Plan envelope and constraints

Scope is fixed: straight overlapping waistband, closure below the band, no lining, no gathering, no pockets, no curved waistband [6]. Step order is strictly prerequisite-chained (1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5). Do not reorder steps or assume dimensions not stated in a source-supported instruction [3][4].
