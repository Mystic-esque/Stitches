## Required measurements

The following 14 body measurements are required to generate the Tina faux-wrap top [1]:

- Head circumference
- Biceps circumference
- Chest circumference
- HPS to waist back
- Neck circumference
- Shoulder to shoulder
- Shoulder slope
- Waist to armpit
- Waist to seat
- Seat circumference
- Waist circumference
- Shoulder to wrist
- Waist to underbust
- Wrist circumference

## Material structure

Tina is a **knit** fabric design. Specific stretch percentage, drape, grain direction, yardage, or substitution requirements are not specified in the authored planning records and require source qualification before proceeding. [1]

### Piece notes

- **Overlapping front** (condition: always): cut 2 from main fabric with mirroring — both left and right wrap front panels are included in every layout. [3]
- **Front bottom** (condition: front-bottom): cut 1, no fold, not mirrored — this piece is conditional; it applies only when the front-bottom option is selected. [4]
- **Back** (condition: always): cut 1 on a single fold from main fabric, not mirrored — always present in the layout. [5]
- **Sleeve** (condition: sleeves): cut 2 with mirroring, no fold — sleeves are conditional; they apply only when the sleeves option is selected. [6]

## Cutting caveat

The documented cut list has mirrored fronts, a back on fold, and optional sleeve and front-bottom parts. A generated, checked pattern is still required before cutting. [1]
