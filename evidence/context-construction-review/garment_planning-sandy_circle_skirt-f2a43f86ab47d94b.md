**Condition notes:**
- **default**: skirt cut on a single fold; use when the standard (seamed) configuration is selected.
- **seamless**: skirt cut on a double fold; use when the seamless configuration is selected. The two skirt variants are mutually exclusive — cut one or the other, not both.
- **curved**: cut when a curved waistband is chosen; 2 pieces, no fold.
- **straight**: cut when a straight waistband is chosen; 1 piece, no fold.
- **always**: waistband interfacing is cut regardless of other options; 1 piece from interfacing material.

## Cutting caveat

The documented default and seamless variants use different folds, and the waistband option (curved vs. straight) changes the cut list. A generated, checked layout is still required before cutting. [7]
