# Seam Puckering Diagnosis & Scrap Testing

Seam puckering observation, scrap-test procedure, stitch-release diagnostic, tension-adjustment remedy, conditional sample-only comparison, issue patterns for unresolved puckering, and applicability to Sandy and Tina candidates

## Puckering observation

Ripples along seams may indicate thread tension as a contributing cause. Confirming this requires a controlled test on scrap fabric before altering a finished garment.

## Diagnostic test: Scrap-stitch-release

Use scrap material only. Sew a short sample seam using the same machine, thread, and settings as the garment. Carefully release both threads along a small section (a few centimetres) without pulling or damaging the fabric. Compare the released and still-stitched sections.

**Maker question:** Do the ripples flatten when you carefully remove the stitches?

**Possible outcomes:**

- **Not-tested:** Perform this scrap-stitch-release test first. Do not proceed to a tension comparison until the test is complete.
- **Unchanged:** The cause remains unresolved. Do not recommend a machine adjustment. Other causes include material structure, feeding, and needle/thread choice.
- **Relaxes:** Ripples flatten when stitches are released. Thread tension may be contributing. You may consider a separate lower-tension sample comparison.

**Risk notes:** Do not remove stitches from the actual garment. Stop if the sample fabric is being damaged.

## Conditional remedy: Lower-tension sample (eligible only if test result relaxes)

If the scrap-stitch-release test shows ripples flatten when stitches are released, try adjusting tension on another scrap seam only. Do not alter or unpick the finished garment.

**Safe action:** On another scrap seam, try a modestly lower upper-thread tension while maintaining a balanced stitch. Change one setting at a time and compare it with the original sample.

**Confirmation check:** Does the new sample lie flatter while both sides still form a balanced, secure stitch? If not, stop this path and seek another cause.

**Ineligibility:** If the scrap-stitch-release test result is not-tested or unchanged, the lower-tension comparison is not eligible. Return to the test or seek another cause.

## Applicability

This diagnostic applies to Sandy circle skirt and Tina faux-wrap top candidates. All procedures and outcomes are pending review.
