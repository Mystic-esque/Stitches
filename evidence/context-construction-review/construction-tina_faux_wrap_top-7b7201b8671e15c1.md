# Tina Faux-Wrap Top Construction Sequence

Eight ordered construction steps for the Tina top: sleeve markings, optional dart, shoulder seams, neckline binding, flat sleeve setting, front overlap study, side/underarm seams, and finish planning

The Tina faux-wrap top construction plan (`companion-making-tina-v1`) is a learning plan with explicitly confirmed dart, sleeve, and lower-front branches. Binding and finishes depend on the selected source pattern instructions. No cut-ready geometry or physical review has been completed. All eight steps carry **pending** review status — this plan supports education and planning only, not cut-ready making.

For Tina piece definitions and measurement requirements, see `garment_planning/tina_faux_wrap_top`. For seam puckering diagnostics, see `seam_quality`.

## Construction sequence

Steps are strictly prerequisite-chained. Three steps (dart, sleeves, front-bottom) are conditional — include them only when the authored condition applies. When a conditional step is absent, carry its prerequisites through to the next included step; do not require the omitted physical action.

---

### Step 1 — Read asymmetric markings (ordinal 1, `companion-making-tina-v1-marks`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study the asymmetric notch markings before any other step. The x-notch is at the **back**; the round notch is at the **front**. Pieces: front, back, sleeve.

**Checkpoint:** Can you identify which notch belongs to which edge?

**Prerequisite of:** Understand the optional dart (Step 2).

---

### Step 2 — Understand the optional dart (ordinal 2, `companion-making-tina-v1-dart`)
**Condition:** `dart` (present only if chosen pattern version includes a dart). **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study the dart source instructions before choosing any alternative treatment. Piece: front. Orientation: confirm whether the chosen pattern version has a dart.

**Checkpoint:** Is the dart actually present in your chosen version?

**Prerequisites:** Read asymmetric markings (Step 1).

---

### Step 3 — Join the shoulder relationship (ordinal 3, `companion-making-tina-v1-shoulders`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study both shoulders with a stretch-compatible seam. Pieces: front, back. Orientation: right sides together.

**Checkpoint:** Which edges meet, and which edges remain open?

**Prerequisites:** Understand the optional dart (Step 2). If the dart condition is absent, this step's prerequisite chains back through Step 1.

---

### Step 4 — Follow the neckline binding (ordinal 4, `companion-making-tina-v1-neck`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study how binding ends later enter side or waist finishing. Pieces: front, back. Orientation: binding meets diagonal fronts and back neck, right sides together.

**Checkpoint:** Where will the binding ends be secured?

**Prerequisites:** Join the shoulder relationship (Step 3).

---

### Step 5 — Place the flat sleeves (ordinal 5, `companion-making-tina-v1-sleeves`)
**Condition:** `sleeves` (include only when sleeves are present in chosen version). **Diagnostic:** yes.

Match **x-notch to back** and **round notch to front**. Pieces: front, back, sleeve. Orientation: right sides together; sleeve hem points toward neck opening.

**Checkpoint:** Can you identify the intended notch pairing?

**Prerequisites:** Follow the neckline binding (Step 4).

---

### Step 6 — Understand overlap and lower front (ordinal 6, `companion-making-tina-v1-front-bottom`)
**Condition:** `front-bottom` (include only when lower-front piece is present). **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study the overlap before its lower-front seam. Pieces: front, front-bottom. Orientation: visible outer front faces the lower-front piece.

**Checkpoint:** Which front layer should remain visible?

**Prerequisites:** Place the flat sleeves (Step 5). If sleeves condition is absent, prerequisites chain back to Step 4.

---

### Step 7 — Connect sides and underarms (ordinal 7, `companion-making-tina-v1-sides`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study side seams and, when present, sleeve underseams. Pieces: front, back, sleeve. Orientation: right sides together.

**Checkpoint:** Do all included branches lead here without a missing step?

**Prerequisites:** Understand overlap and lower front (Step 6). If the front-bottom condition is absent, prerequisites chain back to whichever earlier step was last included.

---

### Step 8 — Plan waist and arm finishes (ordinal 8, `companion-making-tina-v1-finishes`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Resolve each finish using the selected source version. Arm finish depends on whether sleeves are included; waist has alternative finishes. Pieces: front, back, sleeve.

**Checkpoint:** Which finish and measurement still need maker review?

**Prerequisites:** Connect sides and underarms (Step 7).

## Plan envelope and constraints

Scope is fixed by `companion-making-tina-v1`: dart, sleeve, and lower-front are explicitly conditional branches confirmed in the plan. Binding and finishes require the selected source pattern instructions and independent maker review. No allowances, exact notch mates beyond the x/round pairing, closures outside the plan, or fit prescriptions are defined here. Unknowns require the selected source pattern or independent maker review. All checkpoints record understanding, not verified sewing or cut readiness.
