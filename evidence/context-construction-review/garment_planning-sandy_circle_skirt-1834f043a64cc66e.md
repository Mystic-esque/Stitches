## Required measurements

The following four body measurements are required to generate the Sandy circle skirt [1]:

- Waist circumference
- Waist to floor
- Waist to hips
- Hips circumference

## Material structure

Sandy is a **woven** fabric design. Specific stretch, drape, grain direction, yardage, or substitution requirements are not specified in the authored planning records and require source qualification before proceeding. [1]

### Configuration variants

- **Default vs. seamless skirt:** These are mutually exclusive options. The default skirt section uses a **single fold**; the seamless variant uses a **double fold**. Only one skirt piece is cut per garment. [3][4]
- **Waistband choice:** Curved waistband (cut 2, no fold) and straight waistband (cut 1, no fold) are mutually exclusive options. [5][6]
- **Waistband interfacing** is cut once from interfacing material under all configurations (condition: always). [7]

## Cutting caveat

The documented default and seamless variants use different folds, and the waistband option changes the cut list. A generated, checked layout is still required before cutting. [1]
