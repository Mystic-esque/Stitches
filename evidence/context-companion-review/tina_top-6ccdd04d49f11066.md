### Condition notes

- **front** and **back** are cut under condition `always` — these are the core pieces for every version of the top.
- **sleeve** is cut only when the `sleeves` option/condition applies: cut 2, mirrored (i.e. left and right), no fold.
- **front-bottom** is cut only when the `front-bottom` condition applies: cut 1, not mirrored, no fold.

### Fold and mirroring detail

- The **back** is cut on a single fold (cut 1 on fold); it is not mirrored.
- The **overlapping front** pieces are not cut on fold; the 2 copies are mirrored to produce left and right wrapping fronts.
- Both **sleeve** pieces are mirrored (one left, one right); no fold.
- **Front-bottom** is cut flat, no mirror, no fold.
