# Tina Faux-Wrap Top Construction Sequence

Eight ordered construction steps for the Tina top: sleeve markings, optional dart, shoulder seams, neckline binding, flat sleeve setting, front overlap study, side/underarm seams, and finish planning

The Tina construction plan (`companion-making-tina-v1`) is a **learning plan** with eight ordered steps covering the faux-wrap top from markings to finishes. Review status: **pending** for all steps and the plan itself — this plan supports education and planning only, not cut-ready making. Three branches are conditional (dart, sleeves, front-bottom); when a branch is omitted its prerequisites chain through to the next included step. The plan envelope explicitly notes that binding and finishes need the selected pattern instructions, and no cut-ready geometry or physical review has been completed.

For piece specifications, measurements, and material requirements, see `garment_planning/tina_faux_wrap_top`. For seam puckering diagnostics, see `seam_quality`.

## Construction sequence

Steps are listed in ordinal order. All checkpoints record understanding, not verified sewing or cut readiness.

---

### Step 1 — Read asymmetric markings (`companion-making-tina-v1-marks`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** no.

Study the sleeve notch markings to distinguish front from back. The x-notch is at the sleeve back; the round notch is at the sleeve front.

**Checkpoint:** Can you distinguish the sleeve front from its back?

**Prerequisite:** *(none — starting step)*

---

### Step 2 — Understand the optional dart (`companion-making-tina-v1-dart`)
**Condition:** dart (conditional branch). **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study the dart's source instructions before choosing an alternative treatment. Piece: `front`. Orientation: confirm whether your chosen pattern version includes a dart.

**Checkpoint:** Is the dart actually present in your chosen version?

**Prerequisite:** Read asymmetric markings (`companion-making-tina-v1-marks`).

*When the dart branch is absent, its prerequisite (Step 1) carries through to Step 3.*

---

### Step 3 — Join the shoulder relationship (`companion-making-tina-v1-shoulders`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study both shoulders with a stretch-compatible seam. Pieces: `front`, `back`. Orientation: front and back right sides together.

**Checkpoint:** Which edges meet, and which edges remain open?

**Prerequisite:** Understand the optional dart (`companion-making-tina-v1-dart`).

---

### Step 4 — Follow the neckline binding (`companion-making-tina-v1-neck`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study how binding ends later enter side or waist finishing. Pieces: `front`, `back`. Orientation: binding meets diagonal fronts and back neck, right sides together.

**Checkpoint:** Where will the binding ends be secured?

**Prerequisite:** Join the shoulder relationship (`companion-making-tina-v1-shoulders`).

---

### Step 5 — Place the flat sleeves (`companion-making-tina-v1-sleeves`)
**Condition:** sleeves (conditional branch). **Diagnostic:** yes.

Match x-notch to back and round notch to front. Pieces: `front`, `back`, `sleeve`. Orientation: right sides together; sleeve hem points toward neck opening.

**Checkpoint:** Can you identify the intended notch pairing?

**Prerequisite:** Follow the neckline binding (`companion-making-tina-v1-neck`).

*When the sleeves branch is absent, its prerequisite (Step 4) carries through to Step 6.*

---

### Step 6 — Understand overlap and lower front (`companion-making-tina-v1-front-bottom`)
**Condition:** front-bottom (conditional branch). **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study the overlap before its lower-front seam. Pieces: `front`, `front-bottom`. Orientation: visible outer front faces the lower-front piece.

**Checkpoint:** Which front layer should remain visible?

**Prerequisite:** Place the flat sleeves (`companion-making-tina-v1-sleeves`).

*When the front-bottom branch is absent, its prerequisite (Step 5, or Step 4 when sleeves is also absent) carries through to Step 7.*

---

### Step 7 — Connect sides and underarms (`companion-making-tina-v1-sides`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study side seams and, when present, sleeve underseams. Pieces: `front`, `back`, `sleeve`. Orientation: front and back right sides together.

**Checkpoint:** Do all included branches lead here without a missing step?

**Prerequisite:** Understand overlap and lower front (`companion-making-tina-v1-front-bottom`).

---

### Step 8 — Plan waist and arm finishes (`companion-making-tina-v1-finishes`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Resolve each finish using your selected source version. Pieces: `front`, `back`, `sleeve`. Orientation: arm finish depends on sleeves; waist has alternative finishes.

**Checkpoint:** Which finish and measurement still need maker review?

**Prerequisite:** Connect sides and underarms (`companion-making-tina-v1-sides`).

---

## Plan envelope and constraints

The plan (`companion-making-tina-v1`) scope is fixed to learning: binding and finishes require the selected pattern instructions; no allowances, exact notch mates outside the x-notch/round-notch pairing, or fit prescriptions are defined here. Unknown geometry and allowances require the selected source pattern or independent maker review. All checkpoints record understanding, not verified sewing or cut readiness. The plan cites FreeSewing Tina top documentation (`companion-source-tina-20261001`) at https://freesewing.eu/docs/designs/tina/instructions/.
