## Required measurements

The following 4 body measurements are required to generate the Sandy circle skirt, per Sandy circle skirt (`companion-candidate-sandy-20261001`):

- Waist circumference
- Waist to floor
- Waist to hips
- Hips circumference

## Material structure

Sandy is a **woven** fabric design, per Sandy circle skirt (`companion-candidate-sandy-20261001`). Specific grain direction, drape, ease, yardage, stretch requirements, or substitution rules are not authored in the planning records and require source qualification before proceeding.

### Piece notes

- **Skirt section** (condition: default): cut 1 on a single fold from main fabric, not mirrored — present when the default (seamed) skirt variant is selected, per Skirt section (`companion-piece-sandy-skirt-default-20261001`).
- **Seamless skirt section** (condition: seamless): cut 1 on a double fold from main fabric, not mirrored — present when the seamless variant is selected; the double fold eliminates the seam, per Seamless skirt section (`companion-piece-sandy-skirt-seamless-20261001`). The default and seamless skirt pieces are **mutually exclusive** variants.
- **Curved waistband** (condition: curved): cut 2, no fold, not mirrored — present when the curved waistband option is chosen, per Curved waistband (`companion-piece-sandy-waistband-curved-20261001`).
- **Straight waistband** (condition: straight): cut 1, no fold, not mirrored — present when the straight waistband option is chosen, per Straight waistband (`companion-piece-sandy-waistband-straight-20261001`). The curved and straight waistband pieces are **mutually exclusive** options.
- **Waistband interfacing** (condition: always): cut 1, no fold, from interfacing material — included in every layout regardless of waistband type, per Waistband interfacing (`companion-piece-sandy-waistband-interfacing-20261001`).
