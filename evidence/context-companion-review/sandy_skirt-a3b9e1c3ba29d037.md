# Sandy Circle Skirt Planning

Sandy circle skirt planning candidate: piece definitions, fold/cut choices, waistband variants (curved, straight, seamless), measurement requirements, material constraints, and pending-review status

Sandy circle skirt is a planning candidate (`companion-candidate-sandy-20261001`) sourced from the FreeSewing Sandy Circle Skirt documentation by Erica Alcusa Sáez [1]. All records carry **pending** review status — this candidate supports education and planning only, not maker-approved cutting [1][7].

## Required measurements

Four measurements are needed before any layout can be generated [7]:

- Waist circumference
- Waist to floor
- Waist to hips
- Hips circumference

## Material constraint

The design requires a **woven** fabric structure [7].

## Pieces and conditional cut list

The cut list varies by waistband option chosen and whether the seamless variant is used. A generated, checked layout is still required before cutting — the default and seamless variants use different folds, and the waistband choice changes the piece count [7].

| Piece | Condition | Cut count | Fold | Material |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Skirt section (`skirt-default`) | default | 1 | single | main |
| Seamless skirt section (`skirt-seamless`) | seamless | 1 | double | main |

### Fold notes

- **Default skirt** (`skirt-default`): cut on a single fold [2].
- **Seamless skirt** (`skirt-seamless`): cut on a double fold — this variant eliminates the back seam [4].
- **Waistband pieces** (curved, straight, interfacing): all cut flat with no fold [3][5][6].

### Waistband variants (mutually exclusive conditions)

- **Curved** (`waistband-curved`): condition `curved`; yields 2 cut pieces from main fabric, not mirrored [3].
- **Straight** (`waistband-straight`): condition `straight`; yields 1 cut piece from main fabric [5].
- **Interfacing** (`waistband-interfacing`): condition `always`; 1 piece cut from interfacing regardless of which waistband style is chosen [6].

No piece in this candidate is mirrored [2][3][4][5][6].

## Cutting caveat

The documented default and seamless variants use different folds, and the waistband option changes the cut list. A generated, checked layout is still required before cutting [7].

## Review status

All pieces and the candidate record itself are **pending review**. These records support education and planning; they are not maker-approved patterns and must not be treated as cut-ready without measured, printed, and maker-checked evidence [1][2][3][4][5][6][7].
