# Fabric & Material Constraints

Woven fabric behavior, grain direction, shrinkage, layout requirements, material type declarations, and review caveats for material records

Both current planning candidates carry a **pending review** status and represent plan-only records — not maker-approved patterns or cut-ready instructions. Material structure and measurement requirements below are exactly as authored in each candidate record.

## Sandy Circle Skirt (`companion-candidate-sandy-20261001`)

- **Material structure:** woven
- **Required measurements:**
  - Waist circumference
  - Waist to floor
  - Waist to hips
  - Hips circumference
- **Cutting caveat:** The documented default and seamless variants use different folds, and the waistband option changes the cut list. A generated, checked layout is still required before cutting.
- **Source:** FreeSewing Sandy Circle Skirt documentation (`companion-source-sandy-20261001`)

Stretch percentage, grain direction, drape, ease, shrinkage, yardage, and layout rules beyond the caveat above are not specified in this record and require source qualification before use.

## Tina Faux-Wrap Top (`companion-candidate-tina-20261001`)

- **Material structure:** knit
- **Required measurements:**
  - Head circumference
  - Biceps circumference
  - Chest circumference
  - HPS to waist back
  - Neck circumference
  - Shoulder to shoulder
  - Shoulder slope
  - Waist to armpit
  - Waist to seat
  - Seat circumference
  - Waist circumference
  - Shoulder to wrist
  - Waist to underbust
  - Wrist circumference
- **Cutting caveat:** The documented cut list has mirrored fronts, a back on fold, and optional sleeve and front-bottom parts. A generated, checked pattern is still required before cutting.
- **Source:** FreeSewing Tina top documentation (`companion-source-tina-20261001`)

Required stretch percentage, inherent stretch behavior, grain direction, drape, ease, shrinkage, substitution guidance, yardage, and layout rules are not specified in this record and require source qualification before use.

## Review and use status

Both candidates are pending review. These records support education and planning only. Do not treat the material declarations, measurement lists, or cutting caveats as confirmed maker guidance or as authorisation to cut fabric. Individual piece cut/fold specifications are out of scope here; see the relevant piece-level entries for those details.
