# Sandy Circle Skirt Construction Sequence

Ordered construction steps for the Sandy circle skirt: closure seam, opening orientation, straight waistband attachment, waistband finishing and fastening, hem finish options, and pending-review status

The Sandy circle skirt construction plan (`companion-making-sandy-v1`) is a learning plan for a non-seamless skirt with a straight overlapping waistband and a closure positioned below the band. The plan envelope explicitly excludes lining, gathering, pockets, and curved waistband variants. No physical review has been completed. All five steps carry **pending** review status — this plan supports education and planning only, not cut-ready making.

For Sandy piece definitions and measurement requirements, see `garment_planning/sandy_circle_skirt`. For seam puckering diagnostics, see `seam_quality`.

## Construction sequence

Steps are strictly prerequisite-chained in ordinal order. All five steps carry `condition: always` — none are conditional branches. Pieces involved are `skirt-default`, `waistband-straight`, and `waistband-interfacing` as specified per step.

---

### Step 1 — Understand the closure seam (ordinal 1, `companion-making-sandy-v1-closure-seam`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study the seam ending at the opening. This plan explicitly excludes seamless skirts. Piece: `skirt-default`. Orientation: right sides face each other. Edge: `sandy.skirt/opening`.

**Checkpoint:** Can you point to the opening that must stay accessible?

---

### Step 2 — Plan the opening (ordinal 2, `companion-making-sandy-v1-opening`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

This study uses a closure below the waistband — not a zipper through it. Piece: `skirt-default`. Orientation: the opening remains above the closed seam.

**Checkpoint:** Is your intended closure outside this study? Pause rather than guess.

**Prerequisite:** Understand the closure seam (Step 1).

---

### Step 3 — Connect skirt and straight band (ordinal 3, `companion-making-sandy-v1-band`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Study the straight-band attachment and its overlap. Curved bands, lining, and gathering are outside this plan's scope. Pieces: `skirt-default`, `waistband-straight`, `waistband-interfacing`. Orientation: interfaced band side and skirt, right sides together. Edges: `sandy.skirt/waist`, `sandy.waistband/attachment`.

**Checkpoint:** Identify the inside band edge and the overlap before proceeding.

**Prerequisite:** Plan the opening (Step 2).

---

### Step 4 — Understand the waistband finish (ordinal 4, `companion-making-sandy-v1-band-finish`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

Choose a source-supported overlap fastening. Do not infer dimensions from this diagram — no allowances or closure dimensions are defined in this plan. Piece: `waistband-straight`. Orientation: the inside band edge covers the attachment.

**Checkpoint:** Write down any unresolved closure or allowance detail.

**Prerequisite:** Connect skirt and straight band (Step 3).

---

### Step 5 — Compare hem finishes (ordinal 5, `companion-making-sandy-v1-hem`)
**Condition:** always. **Diagnostic:** yes.

The source offers binding and folded-hem approaches. This study does not select an allowance; the choice requires maker review against the selected source pattern. Piece: `skirt-default`. Orientation: inspect the lower edge, not the waist edge. Edge: `sandy.skirt/hem`.

**Checkpoint:** Can you explain which finish you will ask a maker to check?

**Prerequisite:** Understand the waistband finish (Step 4).

## Plan envelope and constraints

Scope is fixed by `companion-making-sandy-v1`: the plan covers only a non-seamless skirt with a straight overlapping waistband and a closure below the band. Lining, gathering, pockets, curved waistband, and zipper-through-band closures are explicitly outside scope. No allowances, exact closure dimensions, or fit prescriptions are defined here. Unknowns require the selected source pattern or independent maker review. All checkpoints record understanding, not verified sewing or cut readiness.
