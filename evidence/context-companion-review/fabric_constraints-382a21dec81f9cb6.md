## Material structure implications

The two candidates use structurally different fabric types:

| Candidate | Material structure | Key fabric behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Sandy circle skirt | Woven | Grain direction critical; no inherent stretch; layout fold choices affect cut list |
| Tina faux-wrap top | Knit | Stretch required for fit; grain and stretch direction affect drape and ease |

Because Sandy uses **woven** fabric, grain direction governs how skirt panels hang and how layout folds are specified — the default versus seamless variants each prescribe different fold approaches, making fold choice a cutting decision, not an aesthetic one. Because Tina uses **knit** fabric, the material must have sufficient stretch to accommodate the wrap and fit across the 14 required body measurements.
