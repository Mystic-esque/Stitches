## Build preferences and unit of work

- **Mode:** autonomous implementation; no routine “look at it” stop after each package. Ask only when authority, external access, rights or physical maker evidence truly blocks dependent work.
- **Verification:** every package has real domain/integration/browser checks and live-versus-fixture labels. Commit coherent tested milestones locally; never push or deploy automatically.
- **Cadence:** W01–W10 are milestone packages, not a claim that each is a 15-minute task. Break each active package into small five-field tasks (spec reference, concrete work, acceptance, verification) in STATUS before coding. Keep exactly one next executable task.
- **Submission wow moment:** a maker at a specific step says “this doesn't line up,” Stitches uses their pattern/material/progress plus Sanity's shared graph/KB to ask a discriminating check, explains a safe next action and remembers the outcome. The pattern-connected cutting/assembly view makes the knowledge legible.
