## Source and digital review

- [ ] Confirm source and generated-output rights for this exact design/package, including attribution and redistribution. Upstream code license alone is not a complete rights conclusion.
- [ ] Compare every piece count, fold, grain, material and condition to the selected source. Sandy's curved-band count conflict is unresolved and excluded from its narrow engine study.
- [ ] Confirm each actual edge mate, side/facing orientation, closure position, interfacing, seam allowance and marking. Unknown mates must stay unknown; no diagram inference.
- [ ] Review the making graph's conditional prerequisites and explanations. Disabled Tina branches must not become mandatory operations. Current checkpoint events record study review only.
- [ ] Verify the shared scrap test's applicability, discrimination, safety, conditional comparison and stop path. A symptom or improvement must not become a confirmed cause or finished-garment prescription.
- [ ] Review at least two distinct inputs and bounds for every supported drafting operation. Research precision is not a physical sewing tolerance.

## Physical gates — only after approved printable artifacts exist

- [ ] Record printer/paper/scale, calibration dimensions actually measured, page/assembly registration and effective printable area.
- [ ] Record actual test fabric, layout width/length, fold/selvage/grain, seam allowances, notches and cutting observations.
- [ ] Record join consistency and assembly failures; photograph the actual test garment with consent. An illustration or synthetic rendering is not physical evidence.
- [ ] Record actual fit observations and limits for the specific body/variant/material only. Do not generalize one trial to all sizes or garment families.
- [ ] Rehearse a failed or uncertain step safely on sacrificial material, record the result and recovery or escalation. Do not deliberately damage a finished garment for a demo.

## Intended-user sessions

Ask three consenting intended users to identify a piece/fold, explain an affected edge, find the next eligible learning checkpoint, preserve an unresolved question and restore a private backup. Record completion without prompting, assistance needed, mistakes, time, accessibility barriers and exact UI/version. Leave counts blank until sessions happen. Use these observations to fix friction; do not substitute test-script success for user research.
